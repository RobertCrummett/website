The series of infinite matrix operators, raised to increasing powers, reminds me of the Neumann series I first learned when solving Fredholm integral equations of the second kind. In that context, the series arose from a generalized geometric series, which converged only when the operator norm of the integral operator was less than one. This memory raised convergence questions in me.

Some Remarks

It turns out the series Volterra integral equations generate always converge. From what we have seen above, you can think of this as being a consequence of the 1/𝑚! factor in the denominator of the series terms. There exist more rigorous proofs of this. but I do not fully understand them yet. Fredholm integral equations do not have this property — convergence must be checked.

The initial value problem posed can be naturally transformed into a Volterra integral equation. Boundary value problems are usually transformed into Fredholm integral equations via Green’s functions.
