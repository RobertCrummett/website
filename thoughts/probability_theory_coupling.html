Probability Theory — Coupling

Below is a basic introduction to the notion of coupling. This is a fundamental concept, with applications reaching into the study of Markov processes and optimal transport problem. This overview will be a simplistic coverage of what I see as the principal elements of this topic.

A coupling of random variables (potentially on different probability spaces) is another random variable whose marginal distributions are equivalent in distribution to the original random variable. Of course, a choice of a coupling random variable is non-unique. All ready you can taste the flavor of the optimal transport problem, if you are familiar with that area of study.
